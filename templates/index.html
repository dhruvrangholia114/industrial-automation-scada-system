PLC Logic Notes

  • AUTO starts pump at or below 30% and stops at or above 80% (hysteresis).
  • Sensor fault or high-high level (95%) inhibits the pump.
  • Low-level alarm is below 20%; high-level alarm is at/above 90%.
  • PLC scan concept: read process inputs → evaluate logic → update output.
